Project · November 2026EngiFlow AI

Author: Jonas Bonnett
What it is: An engineering project workflow app (“CAD AI Assistant — Executive Design Eng.”) — dashboard, project creation, requirements register, CAD, assembly, documentation, quality, risk register, reports, and settings, with AI suggesting requirements and risks
Built with: Base44
Status: Working prototype, explicitly PRELIMINARY — a clean working shell with several sections still conceptual; not a production system

Open the live demo →
Requires a free account — the app asks you to sign in before showing the dashboard.

What it does

An engineering project lives or dies on its workflow: requirements get written down, risks get named, documents get revised, and the design gets reviewed — or none of it happens and the project drifts. EngiFlow AI puts that workflow in one app. A project enters through the dashboard, carries a register of requirements, its CAD and assembly work, its documentation and quality records, and a register of risks, with reports to show where it stands. AI assists rather than decides: it suggests requirements and risks for the project to consider, and the app offers downloadable markdown templates and a draft bill of materials to work from. One workspace holds:

  • Dashboard and project creation — the state of the project at a glance, and new projects entered with their scope recorded.
  • Requirements register — what the project must do, held as a register the design is measured against, with AI-suggested requirements to consider.
  • CAD and assembly — the design work, tied to the same project as the requirements.
  • Documentation and quality — the controlled records and the checks on the work, in the same place as the design.
  • Risk register — risks named, logged, and tracked open until closed, with AI-suggested risks to consider.
  • Reports and settings — reports exportable as CSV, JSON, and markdown, plus downloadable markdown templates and a draft BOM.

The seeded sample project — fictional, labeled PRELIMINARY

The app holds one seeded fictional sample project (“Pouch Cell Holder Assembly”, labeled PRELIMINARY) — a made-up sample created to show the workflow working, not a real project, not a real client, and not a reflection of any real holder program. It was created October 16, 2026, at Revision 0.1, with the owner shown as “Design Eng — Cell Integration”, and it carries 5 requirements and 3 open risks. The sample project carries a PRELIMINARY badge, a status of “Preliminary”, and the description “Preliminary concept for design review” — the app says what it is, and so does this page.

Honest framing

This is a prototype, explicitly preliminary — not finished, and not in production use, and this page does not claim otherwise. The app itself carries a bottom banner reading “PRELIMINARY — NOT RELEASED FOR PRODUCTION”, and several sections are conceptual rather than built out. The single sample project is fictional, seeded only to demonstrate the workflow. EngiFlow AI is a working demonstration of engineering project workflow — dashboard, requirements, CAD, documentation, quality, and risk in one app — not a system in production use.

Why it’s here

The through-line of his work is the same everywhere on this site: document the thing, then improve the process that builds the thing. He has written SOPs, managed blueprint lifecycles and engineering revisions in Windchill, built a Word/Excel tracker for pouch cell holders with pass/fail test results, and trained technicians on repeatable procedures. The Manufacturing Documentation Studio is that discipline turned into a documentation tool; GRANDMASTER AI is the same instinct at the orchestration level. EngiFlow AI is that instinct at the project level: one workflow where requirements, design, documentation, quality, and risk are held together, with AI suggesting what to watch for instead of leaving it to memory.

Skills this demonstrates

Engineering project workflow design across requirements, CAD, documentation, quality, and risk; requirements and risk register discipline; report and template design for engineering handoff; and building a working platform with Base44 — the same instinct as the Documentation Studio, GRANDMASTER AI, JDOGG ROCKSTAR, and Scripted CAD: he builds the systems that run the work.
