Drawing · April 2012Mecanum Wheel Assembly — Exploded View

Author: Jonas Bonnett
Mark: JDOGG ROCKSTAR CORP. — his own work, drawn under his own mark
Sheet: DWG NO 00001 · Size B · Scale 1:1 · Rev 1 · Sheet 1 of 1
Released: Initial release for production, 4/4/2012
Result: A complete exploded assembly drawing — balloons, parts list, revision table, title block

What it is

An exploded assembly drawing of an omni-directional Mecanum wheel drive unit. The wheel's rim carries angled polyurethane rollers — the geometry that gives the wheel its sideways motion as well as its forward roll, the core of omnidirectional robot drive. The sheet shows the whole unit taken apart along its assembly axis: hub, wheel plates, rollers and bearings, and the fasteners that hold them together.

The documentation

Six balloon callouts tie every part in the view to the parts list, which gives material and quantity for each item. A revision history table records the initial release, and the title block carries the drawing number, size, scale, and revision. It is a complete example of professional assembly documentation — the same discipline that runs through his later work.

Why it's here

Drawn in April 2012 under his own JDOGG ROCKSTAR CORP. mark, this sheet predates every employer under NDA. It carries no employer name, no proprietary detail, and no third-party marks — it is his work, start to finish, and safe to publish in full.

A related 2012 sheet

The cover of his 2023 research study shows a second sheet from the same period — "Gear and Wheel Assembly" (DWG 05072012, drawn 5/7/2012), a gear-and-motor drive assembly with an 18-item parts list. That sheet is filed with the study as source material for the battery R&D book; it is not reproduced here.

Skills this demonstrates

Exploded assembly documentation, balloon callouts tied to a parts list, materials and quantities per item, revision control, and title-block practice — the drawing fundamentals behind every assembly he has documented since.
