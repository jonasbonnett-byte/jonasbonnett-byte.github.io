Project · November 2026Manufacturing Documentation Studio

Author: Jonas Bonnett
What it is: A documentation-management workspace prototype for manufacturing — a controlled document register with a status workflow, manufacturing travelers, inspection records, bills of materials, nonconformance reports, change tracking, and training attention items
Built with: Base44, deployed on Netlify
Cost: $0 — no paid services or API keys
Status: Working prototype with a fictional sample workspace loaded — not a production system

Open the live demo →

What it does

On a shop floor, the document is the process: if the work instruction is out of date, the build is out of date. The Manufacturing Documentation Studio is a workspace that keeps controlled documents, their revision status, and the records that prove the work happened — in one place, instead of scattered across shared drives and inboxes. One workspace holds:

  • Controlled document register — every document with a revision and a status in the workflow: Draft → In Review → Approved → Superseded → Obsolete.
  • Manufacturing travelers — the step-by-step record that follows a job through the floor.
  • Inspection records — what was checked, against what, and whether it passed.
  • Bills of materials — what goes into the assembly, revision-controlled like everything else.
  • Nonconformance reports — defects and discrepancies, logged open and tracked until closed.
  • Change tracking and training attention items — pending changes and the training items that need someone’s attention before work proceeds.

The sample workspace — Northstar Prototype Works

The live demo carries a fictional sample workspace, loaded November 11, 2026, for a made-up company called Northstar Prototype Works. It is the whole point of the demo: open it and the workspace is already populated, so a visitor can see the register, the workflow, and the records working instead of an empty shell. What is in it:

  • 22 records in total, including 8 controlled documents — Draft 4, In Review 3, Approved 1, Superseded 0, Obsolete 0.
  • 2 open inspections, 2 active nonconformances, and 1 pending change.

The sample documents, all Revision A:

  • TR-NPW-001 — Manufacturing traveler, “WO-NPW-1042” — In Progress

Honest framing

This is a prototype, not a production system, and this page does not claim otherwise. All sample data is fictional — Northstar Prototype Works is a made-up company, and no record in the demo describes real work, a real employer, or a real product. The app itself carries a disclaimer: it is a documentation-management prototype that does not independently validate engineering designs or certify regulatory compliance. Users must independently verify technical requirements, drawings, material specifications, safety controls, inspection methods, and approval status before production use. The studio is a working demonstration of the documentation discipline — revision control, status workflow, travelers, inspections, and nonconformances — not a system in production use.

Why it’s here

The through-line of his work is the same everywhere on this site: document the thing, then improve the process that builds the thing. He has written SOPs, managed blueprint lifecycles and engineering revisions in Windchill, built a Word/Excel tracker for pouch cell holders with pass/fail test results, and trained technicians on repeatable procedures. The Manufacturing Documentation Studio is that discipline turned into a tool of his own — the register, the workflow, and the records, built the way he believes they should work on a real floor.
