Project · November 2026EngiMatrix Executive

Author: Jonas Bonnett
What it is: An executive orchestration console — 16 specialist agents across 9 departments, an executive dashboard (KPIs, projects, strategy), Projects + Workspace, and 7 stage gates from G0 Idea & Intake through G6 Successful handover
Built with: Base44
Status: Early-stage preliminary prototype — a polished shell, lightly populated, with the flagship project still at Gate G1; not a production system

Open the live demo →
Requires a free account — the app asks you to sign in before showing the dashboard.

What it does

An executive does not manage a project by opening its CAD file — they manage it by its gates, its numbers, and its decisions. EngiMatrix Executive puts that view in one console: a dashboard of KPIs, projects, and strategy, with 16 specialist agents spread across 9 departments and a 7-gate stage process a project walks from idea to handover. The console is built for the question an executive actually asks — where does this stand, what is next, and who owns it — rather than the detail of any single discipline. One workspace holds:

  • Executive dashboard — KPIs, projects, and strategy at a glance, the state of the portfolio instead of the state of one drawing.
  • 16 specialist agents across 9 departments — the console’s working staff, organized by department, available to the projects it oversees.
  • Projects + Workspace — the projects under management and the workspace where their work is held.
  • 7 stage gates — G0 Idea & Intake, G1 Research & Requirements, G2 Concept & Feasibility, G3 Design & Development, G4 Validation & Testing, G5 Production Readiness, and G6 Successful handover — the path a project walks before it is handed over.
  • Console exchanges — the running record of work done through the console.

The sample project — fictional, preliminary

The console holds one fictional illustrative sample project (“JB-ENG-001 Pouch Cell Holder Assembly”, preliminary) — a made-up sample created to show the console working, not a real project, not a real client, not a real project code, and not a reflection of any real holder program. The sample project sits at Intake, at Gate G1 (Research & Requirements), and the console around it is lightly populated: 1 active project, 16 agents, 2 console exchanges, and 0 open tasks. The sample is there to demonstrate the gates and the dashboard — nothing in it describes real work.

Honest framing

This is the least built-out of the three Base44 pages on this site, and this page says so plainly rather than dressing it up. EngiMatrix Executive is a polished shell at an early stage: the flagship project is still at Gate G1, the later gates are pending, and the decision log and risk register are deferred — not yet wired in. The app’s own build brief calls it a “preliminary portfolio concept”, and that is how it is treated here: preliminary throughout, not finished, and not in production use, and this page does not claim otherwise. What exists is real — the dashboard, the agents, the gates, and the sample project moving through them — but it is a beginning, not a completed system.

Why it’s here

The through-line of his work is the same everywhere on this site: document the thing, then improve the process that builds the thing. He has written SOPs, managed blueprint lifecycles and engineering revisions in Windchill, built a Word/Excel tracker for pouch cell holders with pass/fail test results, and trained technicians on repeatable procedures. The Manufacturing Documentation Studio is that discipline turned into a documentation tool; GRANDMASTER AI is the same instinct at the orchestration level and EngiFlow AI at the project level. EngiMatrix Executive is that instinct at the executive level: the gates, the KPIs, and the agents an executive needs to see before deciding what moves forward — the view from above the work, built while the work underneath is still being built.

Skills this demonstrates

Executive-level project thinking — stage gates from idea to handover, KPIs, and strategy; multi-agent orchestration across departments; dashboard design for decision-making rather than detail; and building a working platform with Base44 — the same instinct as the Documentation Studio, GRANDMASTER AI, EngiFlow AI, JDOGG ROCKSTAR, and Scripted CAD: he builds the systems that run the work.
