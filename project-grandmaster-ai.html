Project · November 2026GRANDMASTER AI

Author: Jonas Bonnett
What it is: An engineering-intelligence command center built around a 12-agent AI council — project intake, requirements, CAD/BOM, manufacturing, quality, an executive dashboard, agent assignments, tasks, risks and approval gates
Built with: Base44
Status: Working prototype, mid-build — populated with live projects, not a production system

What it does

An engineering project touches a dozen disciplines at once — programme management, CAD, manufacturing, quality, safety — and the work of keeping them coordinated is itself a job. GRANDMASTER AI puts that coordination in one place: a command center where a project enters through intake, carries its requirements, its CAD and BOM, its manufacturing and quality work, and its risks and approval gates, all under an executive dashboard. A 12-agent AI council — Programme Management, CAD, AI/ML, Manufacturing, Quality, Safety, and more — works the project alongside him: agents take assignments, run tasks, and log their work, so the state of a project is visible instead of scattered across inboxes and spreadsheets. One workspace holds:

  • Project intake — a project enters the system with its scope and purpose recorded.
  • Requirements — what the project must do, held as a register the design is measured against.
  • CAD/BOM — the design and its bill of materials, tied to the same project as the requirements.
  • Manufacturing and quality — the build and the checks on it, in the same place as the design.
  • Executive dashboard — the state of every project at a glance: tasks, risks, priorities, gates.
  • Agent assignments, tasks and risks — work assigned to council agents, tracked open until closed, with risks logged the same way.
  • Approval gates — the stage gates a project passes through before it moves forward.

Populated and working — not an empty shell

The command center is in use, not a mock-up. As of November 11, 2026, it holds:

  • 2 active projects — “Electric Longboard” (Concept) and “Precision Mechanical Assembly — Concept-to-Release Demonstration (Internal R&D illustrative)”, with activity recorded October 15–23, 2026.
  • 6 open tasks, 8 open risks, 5 requirements, and 7 project priorities.
  • 29 agent runs — the council working real assignments, with the runs logged.

Build phases — what is live, what is planned

GRANDMASTER AI is mid-build, and this page says which parts are live and which are still ahead, because a prototype that pretends to be finished is worse than an honest one. Phases 1–2 are active: the council, the executive view, requirements, and design review. Phase 3 — 3D Visualization and Video Studio — is planned next. Phase 4 — Data & Analytics and Budgets — follows, and Phase 5 — Quality & Release — after that. The polished dark-navy interface is real, and so is the work underneath it; the phases that are not built yet are named here, not hidden.

Honest framing

This is a prototype, mid-build — not finished, and not in production use, and this page does not claim otherwise. AI outputs inside the app are labeled preliminary/illustrative throughout; costs shown are estimates, not supplier quotations; and no AI output sets approval or release state — a human decides that. The two projects in the app are his own work and an explicitly illustrative demonstration; no record in it describes a real employer or a real production program. GRANDMASTER AI is a working demonstration of engineering orchestration — intake, requirements, design, manufacturing, quality, and executive oversight in one command center — not a system in production use.

Why it’s here

The through-line of his work is the same everywhere on this site: document the thing, then improve the process that builds the thing. He has written SOPs, managed blueprint lifecycles and engineering revisions in Windchill, built a Word/Excel tracker for pouch cell holders with pass/fail test results, and trained technicians on repeatable procedures. The Manufacturing Documentation Studio is that discipline turned into a tool of his own; JDOGG ROCKSTAR and Scripted CAD are the same instinct — he builds systems and automates the tools, including his own work. GRANDMASTER AI is that instinct at the orchestration level: one command center where intake, requirements, design, manufacturing, quality, and executive oversight meet, with an AI council doing the coordination work.

Skills this demonstrates

Engineering project orchestration across intake, requirements, design, manufacturing, and quality; multi-agent AI workflow design — assigning work to specialist agents and tracking it to closure; executive dashboard and stage-gate thinking; and building a working platform with Base44 — the same instinct as the Documentation Studio, the local AI agent, and Scripted CAD: he builds the systems that run the work.
