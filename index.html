AboutFrom the drawing to the shop floor

Jonas brings a Manufacturing Engineering Technology background specializing in Engineering CAD Design/Drafting, with experience as Product Owner and Lead Assembler for the 2Ah Pouch Cell Holder and PDAQ Holder.

With 9+ years of real-world professional experience, he is a versatile Mechanical/CAD Designer and Cell Engineering Technician with hands-on experience in lithium-ion pouch-cell fabrication, electro-mechanical assembly and precision CAD drafting in AutoCAD Civil 3D and PTC Creo Parametric. He bridges digital drafting and shop-floor execution: writing Standard Operating Procedures, designing custom lab fixtures and 3D-printed tooling, managing engineering changes in Windchill, and holding drawings to ASME Y14.5-2009.

He believes process documentation keeps quality from walking out the door with people, which is why he trains technicians and builds repeatable procedures wherever he works.
