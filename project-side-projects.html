Project · November 2026Side Projects — PhoneFresh & DoneToday

Author: Jonas Bonnett
What they are: Two small consumer utilities built outside his engineering work — everyday tools, not engineering projects
Status: Finished small utilities, live and open — no account needed to use either one

These two are here to show breadth, not to pose as engineering work. They are small, finished utilities for everyday life — a phone cleanup helper and a repeatable checklist app — built with the same habit of making a process simple and repeatable that runs through his engineering work, but they are consumer tools, and this page does not present them as anything else.

PhoneFresh — Easy Phone Cleanup

Open PhoneFresh →

PhoneFresh helps clear out a cluttered phone in three simple moves:

  • Find duplicate photos — tap the button, choose some photos from the library, and PhoneFresh spots the ones that look like duplicates — right on the phone. The photos never leave the device.
  • Clean out old emails — pick the email app, follow the steps, and tap Done on each one as you finish.
  • Free up phone space — pick the phone type and follow big, simple steps. Nothing gets deleted without the user saying so.

Its golden rule: if you haven’t opened an app in 6 months, you don’t need it on your phone. You can always download it again later — the account and data are safe.

DoneToday — Your day, organized in one place

Open DoneToday →

DoneToday is a repeatable checklist app: save any repeatable checklist once, reload it anytime with one tap. It has three tabs — Today, Follow-ups, and Templates — and three preset templates to start from:

  • Morning Focus — 3 items.
  • Work Day Shutdown — 4 items.
  • Weekly Reset — 4 items.

Its footer line says it plainly: “DoneToday · your tasks stay on this device.” Tasks are stored on the device, not sent anywhere.

Why they’re here

The through-line is the same one behind the engineering projects on this site: take a messy, repeated process and make it simple enough to do the same way every time. The Manufacturing Documentation Studio does that for controlled documents, GRANDMASTER AI for engineering orchestration, and EngiFlow AI for project workflow. PhoneFresh and DoneToday do it at the smallest scale — a phone and a day. They show the habit, not the discipline: the same instinct, applied to everyday tools.

Skills this demonstrates

Building small, finished utilities for real everyday use; keeping an interface simple enough to use without instructions; and shipping something complete — the same finish-the-thing instinct as the Documentation Studio, GRANDMASTER AI, EngiFlow AI, EngiMatrix Executive, JDOGG ROCKSTAR, and Scripted CAD: he builds the systems that run the work — and the tools that tidy up after it.
